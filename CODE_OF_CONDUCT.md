# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Be patient. Be constructive.

This is a small hobby project. Everyone here — including the maintainer — is learning.

---

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming
- Give constructive feedback, not just criticism
- Be patient with responses — this isn't a full-time project

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Dismissive or condescending comments
- Spam

---

## Enforcement

If something feels off, open an issue or reach out directly.  
I reserve the right to close issues or block users who don't follow these basic rules.

---

*Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), kept simple.*
