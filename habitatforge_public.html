MMid mode — place panels between two grid points
What does Mid Mode do? Lets you place panels between normal grid points. This allows you to add a mid-floor between two full floors, or a mid-wall between two walls — ideal for hamster passages between smaller compartments.

TIPS
If a tube is unmatched, an orange warning appears at the bottom left — click it to focus the camera on the mismatched area.
Filament color can be set per panel — use it to plan colors for your real print.
The ▶ VIEW button hides the sidebar, giving the full area to the 3D view. Click ✕ CLOSE at the top right to exit.
